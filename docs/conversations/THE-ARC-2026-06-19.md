## 1. The Convergence Loop ("understand to comfort")

Eric's stopping condition isn't *functional*, it's *coherent*. Most people stop understanding something when they can use it. He stops when it stops contradicting itself. That higher bar is the engine behind everything — and it's the same algorithm Heady's architecture runs (coherence kernel, consistency bus, embed-then-gate). **Comfort = the loop reaching a fixed point.** Not a feeling chased — a convergence reached.

## 2. The Machine: Detector → Projector → Sorter → Breaker

These aren't four parts. They're one loop:

- **Detector** — high-gain meaning/intent sensor. Picks up *everything*, including faint real signal nobody else catches. Cost: it also catches ghosts (the mixed bag is the signature of a detector tuned high enough to be worth having).
- **Projector** — speaking. Casting an N-dimensional thought as a 1-D shadow (words). Always lossy. "Light tricks": more angles reconstruct the object (CT scan); one clever angle deceives (shadow puppet).
- **Sorter** — the sanity check. Runs constantly. Sorts detector output into *real* vs *rabbit*. This is the part that makes the high-gain detector safe rather than delusional. **It is the thing worth protecting** — it weakens under load (tired, flooded, breaker near trip), and that's exactly when to guard it.
- **Breaker** — trips on **fidelity**, not load. Not "too much current" — "I can't verify the signal left me intact / arrived intact." A near-miss is worse than a clean miss because it *looks* like a hit and leaves the gap open while appearing closed.

## 3. The Relocation Move (the spine of the whole night)
